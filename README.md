# 🎧 Spotify Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing the **Most Streamed Spotify Songs (2023)**. 
The dashboard focuses on streaming trends, audio features, and mood-based insights.

## Dataset
- Source: Kaggle – Most Streamed Spotify Songs 2023
- Features include:
  - Streams
  - Energy
  - Danceability
  - Valence
  - Liveness
  - Explicit content
  - Release year

## Key KPIs & Measures
- Total Streams
- Engagement Quality Index
- Chill Factor
- Mood Score
- Artist Dominance Index
- Hit Potential Score

##Visualizations
- Donut Chart – Stream Share %
- Gauge – Chill Factor
- Area & Stacked Area – Mood trends over time
- Scatter – Energy vs Valence (Mood classification)
- Treemap – Artist dominance

##Insights
- High-energy and positive-valence songs dominate streaming
- Chill music trend shows gradual increase over recent years
- Few artists contribute a major share of total streams
